06 — THE STORY SO FAR

Made for the
everyday you.

GlowVeda began with a simple thought: skincare should be approachable, not intimidating.

Built around the needs of teenagers, college students and young adults, the idea is to bring together affordability, gentle care and ingredient transparency. We're at the beginning of the journey, and we're glad you're here.

With care, Team GlowVeda ✳
07 — GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

Where we are today, in plain words.

When does GlowVeda launch?

We're at the beginning of the journey and haven't announced a launch date yet. Join the early-access list below and we'll share updates as the story unfolds.

What will you be making?

Our planned range focuses on everyday essentials: a gentle cleanser, a daily moisturiser and an everyday lip care. All three are currently in development.

How much will products cost?

We're building GlowVeda to be budget-aware, but final pricing, sizes, formulas and ingredients are yet to be announced.

Is "Find your simple start" a skin assessment?

No. It's a light-hearted way to explore the routine steps on this page. Please choose products based on your own skin needs.
